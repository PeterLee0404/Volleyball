# Long-SSC Depth Jump & Ankle Propulsion Protocol

Training methodology specifically designed to optimize **long stretch-shortening cycle (GCT $\ge 222$ ms)** performance, such as volleyball approach spikes and countermovement depth jumps.

## 1. Technical Execution Cues (Differentiating from Drop Jumps)

| Parameter | Drop Jump (Short-SSC) | Depth Jump (Long-SSC) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Goal** | Minimize GCT, maximize RSI ($\text{Height} / \text{GCT}$) | **Maximize absolute vertical jump height** |
| **Ground Contact Time** | $< 188\text{ ms}$ | $\ge 222\text{ ms}$ (typical $400 - 500\text{ ms}$) |
| **Knee & Hip Excursion** | Stiff, minimal flexion ($< 30^\circ$) | **Deep triple-joint countermovement** ($90 - 100^\circ$ knee flexion) |
| **Athlete Cue** | "Rebound off the ground like a hot stove" | **"Absorb smoothly and explode upward with full arm swing"** |

## 2. Targeted Ankle Plantarflexion Moment Progression

Because peak ankle moment during propulsion correlates higher with jump height ($r = 0.567$) than knee or hip torque, dedicated terminal push-off conditioning is mandatory:

1. **40 cm Countermovement Depth Jump with Arm Swing**:
   - *Drop Height*: 40 cm platform.
   - *Execution*: Step off without jumping up/forward. Land simultaneously on forefoot-to-midfoot, allow compliant deceleration to deep squat (knee flexion $\approx 100^\circ$), immediately reverse into explosive vertical propulsion with maximal ankle plantarflexion.
   - *Volume*: 4 sets $\times$ 5 repetitions.
   - *Rest*: 2 minutes between sets (neuromuscular freshness required).
2. **Band-Resisted Ankle Plantarflexion Hops (Ankle Bounds)**:
   - *Execution*: Resisted rapid triple-extension focusing on terminal ankle snap.
   - *Volume*: 3 sets $\times$ 10–12 repetitions.
   - *Rest*: 60 seconds.

## 3. Kinetic Optimization Focus
- **Do Not Cue High Stiffness**: High vertical stiffness is detrimental in long-SSC jumping ($g = -1.521$). Encourage the athlete to utilize adequate contact duration ($450 - 500\text{ ms}$) to maximize concentric propulsion impulse ($\int (F_z - mg) dt$).
