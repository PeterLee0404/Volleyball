# Muscle structure predictors of vertical jump performance in elite male volleyball players: a cross-sectional study based on ultrasonography

- **Authors**: WeiDong Jiang, Chao Chen, Yilin Xu (2024)
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Physiology (doi: 10.3389/fphys.2024.1427748)
- **Cohort**: 15 elite male volleyball players (Height: 195.4 ± 7.3 cm, Mass: 90.9 ± 9.55 kg, Pro Training: 7.13 ± 3.64 yr; 5 former Chinese national team members).
- **Core Findings**:
  - Rectus Femoris (RF): No significant correlation with any jump type (AJ, CMJ, SJ, DJ).
  - Vastus Lateralis ACSA (VL-ACSA): Strongest predictor across ALL jump types (AJ: R²=0.32, CMJ: R²=0.37, SJ: R²=0.52, DJ: R²=0.25).
  - Lateral Gastrocnemius Fascicle Length (LG-FL): Uniquely and strongly predictive of Attack Jump (AJ) height (R²=0.18, p=0.009), but NOT general jumps (CMJ, SJ, DJ).
  - Combined AJ Prediction Model: VL-ACSA + LG-FL + Training Age yielded R²=0.73 (F=13.86, p<0.001).
- **Practical Recommendations**: Step-ups, single-leg squats, and front squats for VL-ACSA; eccentric calf training for LG-FL lengthening.
