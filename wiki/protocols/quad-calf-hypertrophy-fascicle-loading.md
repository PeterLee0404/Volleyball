# VL-ACSA Hypertrophy & Calf Fascicle Length (LG-FL) Overload Protocol

Targeted resistance training program designed to optimize the two morphological predictors of vertical attack jump height: **Vastus Lateralis Cross-Sectional Area (VL-ACSA)** and **Lateral Gastrocnemius Fascicle Length (LG-FL)**.

## 1. Quadriceps Vastus Lateralis Hypertrophy Block

*Rationale*: High knee extension torque in explosive jumps depends disproportionately on VL rather than RF or VI. EMG and ultrasound trials prove step-ups and front squats produce higher VL activation compared to back squats or leg extensions.

### Exercise Selection & Prescription
1. **Weighted High Step-Ups**:
   - *Setup*: Box height set to 90° knee flexion angle. Focus on driving strictly with the lead leg (minimal back foot push-off).
   - *Loading*: 3–4 sets $\times$ 6–8 reps per leg @ 75–82% 1RM (or RPE 8).
   - *Tempo*: 2-0-X-0 (2s controlled eccentric, explosive concentric).
2. **Front Squats**:
   - *Setup*: Upright torso orientation maximizes anterior chain quadriceps moment arm over posterior chain.
   - *Loading*: 4 sets $\times$ 5–6 reps @ 80–85% 1RM.
   - *Rest*: 2.5–3 minutes between sets.

## 2. Gastrocnemius Fascicle Length (LG-FL) Eccentric Protocol

*Rationale*: Longer fascicles allow faster shortening velocity ($V_{\max}$) in the ankle plantarflexion push-off. Eccentric-only overload stimulates longitudinal sarcomerogenesis (adding sarcomeres in series), increasing fascicle length by 17–19% in 4–6 weeks without increasing pennation angle.

### Exercise Selection & Prescription
1. **Heavy Eccentric Heel Drops (Leg-Straight)**:
   - *Execution*: Position forefeet on a raised deficit block (knee fully locked to isolate gastrocnemius over soleus). Concentric ascent using both legs, transfer load to single leg, and execute slow eccentric descent below horizontal.
   - *Loading*: Bodyweight + 15–30 kg vest/dumbbells; 4 sets $\times$ 8–10 reps.
   - *Tempo*: 0-0-4-0 (4-second full-depth eccentric stretch).
2. **Incline Sled Rebound / Eccentric Catch**:
   - *Execution*: Controlled eccentric deceleration from dorsiflexion under elastic/plate resistance.
   - *Rest*: 90 seconds.

## 3. Programming Integration
- **Microcycle Frequency**: 2 sessions / week (integrated into general physical preparation or early off-season).
- **Concurrent Compatibility**: Place heavy resistance work $\ge 4$ hours separated from high-velocity plyometric jump sessions to avoid acute neural fatigue and stretch-shortening attenuation.
