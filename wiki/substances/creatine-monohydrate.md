# Creatine Monohydrate

Creatine monohydrate is the most rigorously validated nutritional ergogenic aid for enhancing phosphocreatine (PCr) availability, repeated explosive power, and training volume capacity.

## 1. Physiological Mechanism in High-Intensity Exercise
- **ATP-PCr Subsystem**: Catalyzed by creatine kinase ($PCr + ADP + H^+ \leftrightarrow ATP + Cr$), intramuscular phosphocreatine provides the immediate phosphate donor for ATP resynthesis during maximal efforts ($< 10$ seconds).
- **Cellular Hydration & Anabolic Signaling**: Intracellular osmotic pressure increases cell volume, stimulating downstream mTORC1 signaling and satellite cell proliferation to enhance structural remodeling.

## 2. Dosing Protocol & Timing

| Strategy | Dose | Duration | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rapid Saturation** | $20\text{ g/day}$ (split into $4 \times 5\text{ g}$) | 5–7 days | Rapidly saturates muscle creatine stores within one week. |
| **Maintenance** | $3 - 5\text{ g/day}$ (single dose) | Chronic / Indefinite | Maintains elevated intramuscular phosphocreatine levels. |
| **Non-Loading Alternative** | $3 - 5\text{ g/day}$ | 28 days | Reaches equivalent saturation without acute GI distress or sudden water weight gain. |

- **Nutrient Synergy**: Co-ingestion with carbohydrates ($50 - 100\text{ g}$) or carbohydrate-protein blends elevates insulin-mediated sodium-dependent creatine transporter (SLC6A8) uptake.
- **Timing**: Post-workout ingestion slightly outperforms pre-workout due to elevated muscular blood flow and membrane permeability.

## 3. Sports Application in Jumping & Repeated Bouts
- Direct enhancement of repeated jump power ($+5 - 15\%$ total work performed in jump endurance tests).
- Supports resistance-induced hypertrophy of Vastus Lateralis ACSA ([[wiki/concepts/muscle-architecture-jumping.md]]).
