# 8-Week Periodized Volleyball Plyometric Program

An evidence-based 8-week progressive plyometric conditioning regimen validated in competitive adolescent volleyball players (ages 13–14), structured around a 3:1 load-to-deload paradigm.

## 1. Program Parameters

- **Frequency**: 3 sessions / week (non-consecutive days, e.g., Mon-Wed-Fri).
- **Session Duration**: 75–85 minutes:
  - 12–15 min dynamic warm-up (light jog, dynamic stretches, low-amplitude hops).
  - 35–45 min plyometric main block.
  - 5–8 min cooldown.
- **Monitoring Tools**: Foot contacts per session/week, Session RPE (sRPE), heart rate.

## 2. Phase-by-Phase Progression Schedule

```text
Week 1-2: Adaptation ──> Week 3-4: Dev I ──> Week 5-6: Dev II ──> Week 7-8: Sport-Specific
(60-80 contacts)        (80-100 contacts)      (90-110 contacts)       (100-120 contacts)
```

| Phase | Weeks | Focus Drills | Sets × Reps | Contacts / Session | Contacts / Week | Intensity Criteria | Rest Intervals |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adaptation** | 1–2 | Forefoot jumps, low vertical jumps, lateral line hops, skipping | $2 \times 8 - 10$ | 60–80 | 180–240 | 60–75% max effort; strict landing amortization | 30–60 s |
| **Development I** | 3–4 | Squat jumps, tuck jumps, box jumps (30–40cm), repeated hurdle hops (20–30cm) | $2 - 3 \times 8 - 10$ | 80–100 | 240–300 | 70–85% max effort | 45–90 s |
| **Development II** | 5–6 | Repeated vertical jumps, unilateral bounds, zig-zag jumps, approach jumps | $3 \times 6 - 8$ | 90–110 | 270–330 | 80–90% max effort; hurdle/box 30–40cm | 60–120 s |
| **Sport-Specific** | 7–8 | Block jumps, spike jumps, repeated approach jumps, drop jumps (20–40cm), lateral-to-vertical combinations | $2 - 3 \times 6 - 8$ | 100–120 | 300–360 | 85–95% max effort | 90–120 s |

## 3. Measurable Adaptive Outcomes
- **Jump Height**: CMJ net gain $+3.88 \pm 0.44\text{ cm}$ ($F=145.0, p<0.001$).
- **Take-off Force & Power**: Take-off force $+0.12\text{ kN}$; peak concentric power $+5.82\text{ kW}$.
- **Propulsion Speed**: Peak takeoff velocity $+0.29\text{ m/s}$ ($2.66 \to 2.95\text{ m/s}$).
- **Repeated-Jump Endurance**: Endurance Index improved from $1.14 \to 1.30$; spike jump fatigue test error rate dropped by $-1.80 \pm 0.86$.
