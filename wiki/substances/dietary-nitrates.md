# Dietary Nitrates ($NO_3^-$)

Dietary inorganic nitrates (derived from beetroot juice or concentrated sodium/potassium nitrate) enhance athletic performance by modulating the **nitrate–nitrite–nitric oxide ($NO_3^- \to NO_2^- \to NO$)** pathway.

## 1. Physiological Mechanism
- **Vasodilation & Perfusion**: Increases nitric oxide ($NO$) bioavailability under hypoxic and acidic intramuscular conditions, targeting preferentially Type II muscle fibers.
- **Mitochondrial Economy**: Reduces the $O_2$ cost of ATP production by improving mitochondrial $P/O$ ratio (ATP generated per $O_2$ consumed).
- **Excitation-Contraction Calcium Handling**: Elevates sarcoplasmic reticulum calcium release and force production in fast-twitch motor units.

## 2. Dosing Protocol & Timing
- **Acute Dose**: $300 - 600\text{ mg}$ (approx. $5 - 9\text{ mmol}$) of inorganic nitrate (equivalent to 70–140 mL concentrated beetroot juice shot).
- **Timing**: Ingested 2 to 3 hours prior to competition or training (peak plasma nitrite occurs at ~2.5 hours).
- **Chronic Protocol**: 3–7 days of daily loading produces superior contractile enhancement compared to isolated acute intake.
- **Contraindication**: Avoid chlorhexidine-containing mouthwashes, which kill oral commensal bacteria required to reduce nitrate ($NO_3^-$) to nitrite ($NO_2^-$).
