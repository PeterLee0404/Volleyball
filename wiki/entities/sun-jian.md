# Jian Sun

Professor and research lead at Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Human Sports Performance Science, Guangzhou Sport University. Specializes in 3D motion capture, force platform kinetics, and Stretch-Shortening Cycle (SSC) movement mechanics.

## Key Contributions
- Identified the biomechanical characteristics of long-SSC depth jumps (GCT $\ge 222$ ms) in collegiate female volleyball players.
- Uncovered that **lower limb stiffness is inversely related to maximal depth jump height** ($g = -1.521$).
- Highlighted the decisive role of **propulsion-phase velocity and ankle joint plantarflexion moment** over braking-phase variables.
