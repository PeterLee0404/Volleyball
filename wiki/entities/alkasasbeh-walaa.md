# Walaa Jumah Alkasasbeh

Sports physiologist at the Department of Physical Education, School of Sports Sciences, The University of Jordan. Pioneers the integration of Complex Adaptive Systems (CAS) and Network Physiology into high-intensity exercise prescription and athletic fatigue modeling.

## Key Contributions
- Synthesized high-intensity exercise as an emergent, multi-level biological system rather than isolated linear adaptations.
- Modeled the interplay between molecular signaling (mTOR vs AMPK, ROS redox balance), neuromuscular regulation (corticospinal drive vs afferent inhibition), and ergogenic aids.
- Established decision frameworks for synchronizing training modalities (HIIT, resistance, plyometrics) with targeted nutritional modulators.
