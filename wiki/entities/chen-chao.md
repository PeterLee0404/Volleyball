# Chao Chen

Researcher and corresponding author at the School of Competitive Sports, Shanghai University of Sport. Specializes in musculoskeletal ultrasonography, athletic biomechanics, and vertical jump mechanics in elite court athletes.

## Key Contributions
- Quantified in vivo ultrasound architecture (ACSA, MT, PA, FL) of Vastus Lateralis, Rectus Femoris, and Lateral Gastrocnemius in elite national volleyball athletes.
- Discovered that **VL-ACSA and LG-FL together predict 73% of attack jump height variance**.
- Demonstrated that Rectus Femoris architecture is non-predictive of vertical jumping performance due to bi-articular mechanical constraints.
